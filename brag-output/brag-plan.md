# /brag plan — Rabbit Hole

## The answers
- **What is it?** A Chrome extension that, while you watch something on YouTube or Netflix, shows what Reddit is saying about it right now and the Substack writing worth reading next.
- **Who is it for?** Curious people who finish a video and want to go deeper without googling, tab-hopping and guessing.
- **What sets it apart?** It knows what you're watching (no copy-pasting a title), every article tells you *why* it was picked, threads are ranked by momentum rather than raw votes, one click bridges a thread to deeper reading (and an article to the discussion), and it reads nothing until you open it.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Knows what you're watching — and tells you why each article is relevant."
- **Visual hook:** the logo's concentric rings as a tunnel — literally falling into a rabbit hole — that lands inside the product.
- **Real UI to show:** the popup's context bar ("Watching · YouTube"), article cards with the "Highly relevant" label and the "Why this is relevant" box, the Trending Reddit tab with topic and section chips, the "Find deeper reading →" bridge, and the "Private by design" footer.
- **Tone:** `default` — punchy, playful, clean; dark charcoal + electric orange, exactly the product's own palette.
- **Share caption (one line):** Finished a video and want more? Rabbit Hole shows what Reddit is saying about it right now and the Substack writing worth reading next — and tells you why.

## Angle
"Every video has a rabbit hole." The tool is the door. We fall in, land in the real popup, and see the three things it does that a search box can't: it already knows the context, it explains its picks, and it bridges Reddit ↔ Substack. Close on the privacy promise and the tagline.

## Source material and honesty
- **The UI is the real thing:** each popup state is the product's own rendered markup and stylesheet (frozen from the live preview build), animated in place — not a mock-up.
- **Content is illustrative:** the preview's Substack cards are real posts by real writers, so for a public promo they are replaced with fictional sample articles and publications (and the demo channel name with a fictional one). Reddit threads are the project's existing invented sample threads. No invented product claims, numbers or testimonials.
- **Claims used, all true of the build:** knows what you're watching (YouTube / Netflix), Substack picks with a relevance label and a "Why this is relevant" explanation, Trending Reddit sections, thread → reading / article → Reddit bridges, nothing read until you open it. "Open source" is **not** claimed (the repo has no licence yet) — the outro says "on GitHub".

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 22.4 s. Music 120 BPM in D major (I–V–vi–IV), so scene changes land on beats.
